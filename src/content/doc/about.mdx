My name is **Javier Donado**, and I love **Product Engineering**: transforming ideas from concept to reality through technical craft, design thinking, and strategic execution.

I've been a [software craftsman](https://github.com/mrdonado) and lifelong creator since the early 2000s, building systems, exploring ideas, and bringing products to life. Today, I lead technical direction for several digital products within Porsche's Customer Portfolio. Over the years, my work has spanned several [ARTs](https://framework.scaledagile.com/agile-release-train) and product areas, from experiences on [porsche.com](https://www.porsche.com) to products in the My Porsche app. My work combines [solution architecture](/cv) and people leadership, using systems thinking to remove hidden constraints, create environments where teams can own delivery end to end, and make engineering culture a practical advantage.

Beyond coding and architecture, I'm also a [travel photographer](https://instagram.com/jdonadolens) and a longtime [guitarist and composer](https://open.spotify.com/artist/60ICs7yKs1yRwW1ZVIJzYV) — follow along on [Pulse](/pulse) or explore my [**CV**](/cv).
