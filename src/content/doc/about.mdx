My name is **Javier Donado**, and I love **Product Engineering**: transforming ideas from concept to reality with a mix of technical expertise, design thinking, and strategic execution.

I'm a [software craftsman](https://github.com/mrdonado) and lifelong creator, building systems, exploring ideas, and bringing products to life since the early 2000s. Beyond coding and architecture, I'm also a [travel photographer](https://instagram.com/jdonadolens) and a longtime [guitarist and composer](https://open.spotify.com/artist/60ICs7yKs1yRwW1ZVIJzYV) — follow along on [Pulse](/pulse) or explore my [**CV**](/cv).
